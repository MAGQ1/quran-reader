How this app uses the text:
  - uthmani/ holds the text unchanged. It was obtained through
    api.alquran.cloud (edition "quran-uthmani"). The only difference from
    that source is that the Bismillah, which the source attaches to the first
    verse of most surahs, is shown as a separate heading (see bismillah.json).
  - uthmani-shaped/ and shaped-extra.json are the SAME text with its letters
    joined and laid out ahead of time so that older devices can draw it
    (tools/shape-arabic.py). They are a rendering of the text above, not a
    different text: every word is present, in the same order. They are stored
    as private-use characters that only the app's "Quran Shaped" font can draw.
