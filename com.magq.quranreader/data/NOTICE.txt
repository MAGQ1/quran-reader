How this app uses the text:
  - uthmani/ (Uthmani script) and simple/ (Simple, Imla'ei spelling) hold the
    texts unchanged. They were downloaded from tanzil.net ("Text (with aya
    numbers)", with Tanzil's options for pause marks, sajdah and rub el hizb
    signs, superscript alefs, and tatweel below superscript alefs). Tanzil's
    full copyright block, exactly as it came with each download, is in
    TANZIL-HEADER-uthmani.txt and TANZIL-HEADER-simple.txt. The only
    difference from that source is that the Bismillah, which the source
    attaches to the first verse of most surahs, is shown as a separate
    heading (see bismillah.json).
  - uthmani-shaped/, simple-shaped/ and the shaped-extra files are the SAME
    texts with their letters joined and laid out ahead of time so that older
    devices can draw them (tools/shape-arabic.py). They are a rendering of
    the texts above, not a different text: every word is present, in the
    same order. They are stored
    as private-use characters that only the app's "Quran Shaped" font can draw.
