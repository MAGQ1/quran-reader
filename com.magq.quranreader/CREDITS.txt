Arabic text (Uthmani script)
  Tanzil Quran Text - Copyright (C) 2007-2021 Tanzil Project
  https://tanzil.net - Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
  Obtained through api.alquran.cloud (edition "quran-uthmani").
  The text may not be changed, and it is kept unchanged inside the app. The
  only difference from the source is that the Bismillah, which the source
  attaches to the first verse of most surahs, is shown as a separate heading.
  For display on older devices the letters are joined and laid out ahead of
  time (tools/shape-arabic.py); that is a rendering of the same text.
  Full notice: data/NOTICE.txt
