Quran Reader by MAGQ - credits and licenses

Arabic text (Uthmani script)
  Tanzil Project - https://tanzil.net
  Obtained through api.alquran.cloud (edition "quran-uthmani").
  The opening Bismillah, which the source attaches to the first verse of
  most surahs, is shown as a separate heading; the verse text is otherwise
  unchanged. For display on older devices the letters are joined and laid
  out ahead of time (tools/shape-arabic.py); the original text is kept
  unchanged inside the app.

English translation
  Saheeh International (edition "en.sahih" on api.alquran.cloud).

Arabic font
  Amiri Quran - Copyright 2010-2022 The Amiri Quran Project Authors
  (https://github.com/aliftype/amiri)
  Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 - see fonts/OFL.txt
  The app displays Arabic with "Quran Shaped", a modified version of Amiri
  Quran made by tools/shape-arabic.py (letter shapes pre-joined and combined
  into single glyphs so older devices can draw them). It is distributed under
  the same license.
