## One-line summary

Read the Quran in Arabic with an English translation, on your TouchPad.

## Description

Quran Reader is a free, simple and reliable Quran reader for the HP TouchPad.
Everything is included in the app, so it works fully offline.

**Reading**
- The Arabic text in the Uthmani script, with all the vowel marks and stop signs,
  properly joined and laid out for the TouchPad.
- The English translation shown under each verse. Choose between Talal Itani's
  modern English (the default) and the classic translation by Marmaduke Pickthall.
- Choose Arabic-style or regular verse numbers.
- Your reading place is saved, and "Continue reading" takes you straight back.

**Finding your way**
- Browse all 114 surahs or the 30 juz, and search surahs by name or number.
- Works with Just Type: start typing a surah's name in the launcher (for example
  "baq" for Al-Baqara) and tap the result to open it. Switch it on in
  Settings > Just Type.

**Updates**
- The app can tell you when a new version is available in App Museum II.

## Good to know

- After installing (or updating), please restart your TouchPad once (a full restart
  or a Luna restart) so the Arabic font is picked up. The app reminds you if this
  is needed.
- Install through Preware or App Museum II. The Arabic font is installed by the
  package, which needs Preware; installing with palm-install will show the Arabic
  as empty boxes.
- The Arabic text is used unchanged from the Tanzil Project (tanzil.net). Any
  display errors were made by accident: please contact MAGQ on the webOS Archive
  server. May Allah forgive those mistakes.
