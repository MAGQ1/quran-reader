Quran Reader - credits and licenses

Arabic text (Uthmani script)
  Tanzil Project - https://tanzil.net
  Obtained through api.alquran.cloud (edition "quran-uthmani").
  The opening Bismillah, which the source attaches to the first verse of
  most surahs, is shown as a separate heading; the verse text is otherwise
  unchanged.

English translation
  Saheeh International (edition "en.sahih" on api.alquran.cloud).
